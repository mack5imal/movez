If you build or run Movez anyway, expect that it may be incomplete and contain errors. Features may be

every version of Movez, including development and pre-release builds.

## 1. Movez is not medical advice

Movez is a reminder app. It prompts you to take breaks from sitting and suggests movements,
stretches, balance exercises, strength exercises (including some with a kettlebell or dumbbells),
eye exercises and breathing exercises. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate or prevent any
disease, injury or condition. Nothing in Movez is medical, physical therapy, fitness or other
professional advice, and using Movez does not create a doctor–patient, therapist–client, trainer–client
or any other professional relationship with anyone.

The movements and their written instructions are general information for healthy adults. They have
not been reviewed by a doctor, physical therapist or other licensed health professional, and they
cannot account for your health, fitness, history or limitations. Written instructions cannot teach
technique the way an in-person instructor can. Labels in the app that describe what a movement
"targets" or what it is meant to offset describe the intent of the movement, not a promised result.

## 2. Check with a professional first

Talk to a doctor or other qualified health professional before using Movez if you have any health
condition or concern that could make exercise unsafe for you. This includes, but is not limited to,
if you:

- have, or have had, heart disease, chest pain, an irregular heartbeat, high or low blood pressure,
  or a stroke
- get dizzy, faint, or lose your balance easily, or have fallen in the past year
- have a joint, bone, back, neck, shoulder, hip, knee, wrist or hand injury or condition, including
  repetitive strain injury or carpal tunnel syndrome
- have osteoporosis, arthritis or a hernia, or have had surgery recently
- are pregnant or have recently given birth
- have an eye condition, have had eye surgery, or have glaucoma
- have asthma or another breathing condition
- have diabetes or another condition that affects how your body responds to exercise
- take any medication that affects your heart rate, blood pressure, balance or alertness
- have not exercised for a long time, or are unsure what is safe for you

This list is not complete. If you are unsure whether Movez is safe for you, ask a professional before
you start. If you are working with a health professional, follow their advice over anything Movez
suggests.

## 3. Stop if something feels wrong

Stop at once if you feel pain, chest pain or pressure, dizziness or light-headedness, unusual
shortness of breath, an irregular or racing heartbeat, numbness or tingling, blurred or changed
vision, nausea, or anything else that feels wrong. **If symptoms are severe or do not pass quickly,
call 911 or your local emergency number.** Do not start using Movez again until you are sure it is
safe, and if you saw a professional, until they say it is.

A movement should never hurt. Work at an effort you can control from start to finish, and never push
through pain. You can skip or snooze any break, and you can leave out any movement in Settings.

Breathing exercises can make some people light-headed. Do them sitting or standing still, and return
to normal breathing if you feel dizzy. Eye exercises are not a treatment for any eye condition and do
not replace an eye exam.

## 4. Make your space safe

You are responsible for where and how you do each movement. Before you start:

- clear enough space around you, and check the floor is dry and not slippery
- do not use a chair with wheels, or one that swivels or tilts, for any movement that puts weight on
  it; use a stable, fixed chair, or skip the movement
- use only a solid wall, not a cubicle partition, glass, or a door that may open
- for balance movements, stand within reach of something stable you can hold, and stop if you feel
  unsteady
- only do floor movements if you can get down to and up from the floor safely
- if you use a kettlebell, dumbbells or other equipment, choose a weight you can control easily,
  check the equipment is in good condition, and keep a firm grip; never let it fall or swing toward
  anything
- **kettlebell swings are a dynamic movement that carries more risk than the rest of Movez. Do not do
  them unless you have already learned correct technique from a qualified instructor.** Leave them
  out in Settings otherwise
- wear footwear, or go barefoot, in whatever way keeps you stable
- be aware of people, pets and objects around you

Choose the difficulty level and equipment settings that suit you, and leave out anything you are not
comfortable with.

## 6. The app can be wrong

Movez decides when to show a break from your keyboard and mouse activity, the working hours you set,
your Focus settings, and signals such as whether your microphone is in use, a full-screen app is in
front, or your screen appears to be shared. These are estimates. Movez may interrupt you at a bad
moment, miss a break, or hold breaks back when you wanted them. The break screen dims your displays.
Do not rely on Movez for anything where an interruption, or the lack of one, could cause harm, and
do not use it while driving or operating anything that needs your full attention.
